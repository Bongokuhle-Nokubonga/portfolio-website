About me

I'm a hands-on IT technician based in Gauteng. During my internship at Tshwane University of Technology I installed, configured and repaired desktops, laptops, printers and projectors across more than 30 labs, deployed OS images and updates, and traced network faults from the fibre link down to the switch port. I was awarded Top User Service IT Intern 2025/2026.

Before that I worked as a Mainframe Intern, writing and debugging COBOL and JCL on IBM z/OS, and spent three years as an ICT Academic Mentor helping students with networking, cybersecurity and programming. I like problems that need patience and a clear method, and I write things down so the next person can fix them faster.
